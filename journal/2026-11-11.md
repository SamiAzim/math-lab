## Mathematical description of the saved construction

For `LOBES = 4`, set $t=2\pi i/N$. The sampled points come from

$$
\gamma(t)=\left(\cos(t+\tfrac12\sin(4t)),\ \sin t,\ \tfrac12\cos(4t)\right).
$$

The following are consequences of the displayed formula, discussed by the tutor; no independent learner proof was submitted:

- The underlying continuous parametrization is $2\pi$-periodic, since all its trigonometric terms repeat after that parameter increment.
- The height completes four oscillations over one parameter turn. This alone does not establish fourfold spatial symmetry or winding number four.
- The sine phase adjustment vanishes at the height extrema and has greatest magnitude when the height is zero. This follows from the sine/cosine quarter-cycle relationship.
- An explicit parametrized curve can describe ordered motion without a recurrence relating successive points. An explanation of why an interacting system would produce that path remains a separate question.

The extra phase appears only in the x-coordinate. Membership in a particular torus has not been established. Connections to the [original research question](../research/emergent-winding.md) remain open.
