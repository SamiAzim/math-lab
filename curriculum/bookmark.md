# Course bookmark

**Opening chapter · Heat around a ring.** The tutor has proposed Fourier's ring and prepared three lessons. Asked which aspect of a cooling ring they wanted to investigate, the learner said “i don’t know.” The tutor will choose a concrete opening and give them something to think with. No ring calculation or proof has been attempted.

**Resume at “What would you measure?”** The first scene uses four thermometers and asks what information can be recovered from four loose slips. Pick up their reply to that question. If they want the tutor to continue, work one example of labeling a slip and draw the four places. End this first scene once we agree on what a snapshot records. Keep their wording for the next turn. [Opening lessons](lessons/01-heat-around-a-ring.md)

**Detour and return.** A request for a definition pauses at the sentence that needed it. “Zoom out” asks where this question fits in the course. “Let me think aloud” gives the learner space to describe an idea. “Back to the lesson” resumes from the saved sentence. Ordinary language works for each of these.

**Keeping our place.** At a stopping point replace the resume paragraph with the actual last question, the learner's contribution, the next move and any detour still open. Detailed exercise evidence belongs in the journal. Pending threads include a partial derivative from first principles, a separate coding exercise and the notebook's winding construction.
