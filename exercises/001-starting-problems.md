# Initial placement problems

Status: unattempted in this conversation. Spend at most 90 minutes total without looking up solutions. Partial reasoning and precise descriptions of where you get stuck are useful. No solutions are included.

## 1. Functions and proofs

Suppose f:A→B and g:B→C. If g∘f is injective, prove that f is injective. Must g be injective? Prove it or provide a counterexample.

## 2. Linear algebra

Let U,W be subspaces of a real vector space V. Prove that U∪W is a subspace if and only if U⊆W or W⊆U.

## 3. Periodic motion

For real α, let γ(t)=(e^{it}, e^{iαt}). Determine exactly which α allow γ(T)=γ(0) for some T>0. Justify your answer.
